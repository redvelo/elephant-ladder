1. Which country launched Sputnik, the first artificial satellite, in 1957?

Sputnik was Soviet, and it was being second that pushed the United States to set its Moon goal in 1961.

2. According to the Outer Space Treaty of 1967, who may claim the Moon as territory?

Both rivals signed the treaty two years before the landing: whoever arrived first would arrive as a visitor.

3. Why did Armstrong fly the last part of the landing by hand?

The computer kept working through its alarms; it was the boulders at the automatic site that made Armstrong take over, landing with about twenty-five seconds of fuel left.

4. Why did the clearest television pictures of the first steps reach the world through Australia?

Honeysuckle Creek and Parkes happened to have the Moon in view; the signal itself came from the Moon, the dishes only received it.

5. What happened to the Soviet probe Luna 15 during the Apollo 11 mission?

Luna 15 was meant to bring back soil by machine; it crashed while Armstrong and Aldrin were on the surface.
